# Data dictionary

Three layers: **inputs** (the team's draft data), **processed** (measured open data per region) and **app files** (what the app reads). The measured period is **2001-01-01 → 2024-12-31** (24 years, 8,766 days).

## 1. Inputs — `data/inputs/` (team draft, source of truth)

Generated by `make_data.py` (run `python data/inputs/make_data.py`). The four CSVs are the source of truth.

| File | Rows | Key columns |
|---|---|---|
| `sowcast_regions.csv` | 31 | `id`, `country`, `region`, `place` (reference town), `lat`/`lon`, draft climate (`rain_mm_approx`, `mean_temp_c_approx`, `dry_months_approx`), draft soil (`soil_texture`, `drainage`, `slope_pct_approx`), risks 0–3 (`flood_risk_0_3`, `salinity_risk_0_3`, `irrigation_access_0_3`), flags (`tree_crop_dominated`, `soil_degraded`), `data_tier`, `evidence_sources`, `confidence`, `common_crops_in_area` (pipe-separated crop ids) |
| `sowcast_crops.csv` | 21 | traits scored 1–5 (`water_need`, `drought_tolerance`, `flood_tolerance`, `salinity_tolerance`, `sandy_soil_ok`, `erosion_cover`, `organic_matter`, `market`, …), `temp_min_c`/`temp_max_c`, `season_days`, `seasonal_rain_need_mm`, `nitrogen_fixing`, `role`, `pest_notes`, `plain_description` |
| `sowcast_region_crop_fit.csv` | 775 (31 × 25) | `rank`, `fit` (0–95), `fit_band`, parts (`climate_part` … `benefit_part`, 0–1), `common_locally`, `limits` and `why` (semicolon-separated), `confidence`, `trend` (= `tbd`) |
| `sowcast_rotations.csv` | 93 (31 × 3) | `option` (`current`, `alt_a`, `alt_b`), `label`, `sequence` (a pipe separates seasons, `+` = grown together; tokens `fallow`, `perennial_tree`), flags (`has_fallow`, `has_nitrogen_fixer`, `cereal_legume_break`), `avg_crop_suitability`, `source_or_basis`, `confidence` |

## 2. Processed — `data/processed/`

Each of the 31 regions has its own folder named by its id (e.g. `mm-dry-zone/`, `th-isan/`, `vn-mekong/`).

### Cross-site files (start here)

| File | Rows | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `all_sites_annual.csv` | 744 (31 × 24) | One row per region-year with every annual metric. **Best file for a first look.** |
| `all_sites_monthly_climatology.csv` | 372 (31 × 12) | The "typical year" for each region. |

### Per-site files

#### `power_daily.csv` — NASA POWER, daily ✅

| Column | Unit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `tmean_c`, `tmax_c`, `tmin_c` | °C | 2 m air temperature |
| `dewpoint_c`, `rh_pct` | °C, % | humidity |
| `precip_mm` | mm/day | bias-corrected (`PRECTOTCORR`) |
| `wind_ms`, `solar_mj_m2` | m/s, MJ/m²/day | 2 m wind, all-sky shortwave radiation |
| `soil_wet_top` / `soil_wet_root` / `soil_wet_profile` | 0–1 | MERRA-2 soil **wetness** (0 = dry, 1 = saturated) for 0–5 cm, 0–100 cm and the full profile. An index, not volumetric %. |
| `et_model_mm` | mm/day | ⚠ MERRA-2 model ET. Runs low and ignores irrigation. Reference only. |
| `et0_hargreaves_mm` | mm/day | *Derived:* reference evapotranspiration (Hargreaves-Samani, FAO-56 Ra) = crop water **demand** |
| `water_balance_mm` | mm/day | *Derived:* `precip − ET0`. Negative means rain didn't cover demand. |
| `skin_temp_c` | °C | ground-surface temperature |
| `gdd_base10` | °C·day | *Derived:* growing degree days, base 10 °C, cap 30 °C |
| `frost_day` | 0/1 | `tmin < 0 °C` |
| `heat_day` | 0/1 | `tmax ≥ 35 °C` (pollination-damage range for maize and wheat) |
| `heavy_rain_day` | 0/1 | `precip ≥ 20 mm` (ETCCDI "R20mm") |

#### `chirps_daily.csv` — CHIRPS daily rain (ClimateSERV)

`date`, `precip_mm` (mm/day averaged over a 0.1° box around the town). **The rain source for every region** when present.

#### `era5_daily.csv` — ERA5 daily (Open-Meteo)

`date`, `tmax_c`, `tmin_c`, `tmean_c`, `precip_mm`, `et0_fao_mm`. Temperature is used where POWER fails the 2008 step check; the rest is kept for cross-checks.

#### `ndvi_monthly.csv` + `ndvi_mask.json` — NASA VIIRS greenness on cropland ✅

`year`, `month` (2012–2024), `ndvi` (mean over the mask), `n_pixels`, `mask` (`cropland` or `all land`). `ndvi_mask.json` says how many cropland pixels were found in the ~10 km window and where their centre is.

#### `modis_ndvi.csv` — MODIS MOD13Q1, 16-day, 250 m (optional, ORNL)

| Column | Notes |
|---|---|
| `ndvi` | raw NDVI (−0.2…1). Below 0.3 is bare or sparse, 0.3–0.6 is moderate, above 0.6 is a dense green crop. |
| `reliability` | `good`, `marginal`, `snow/ice`, `cloudy` or `fill` |
| `ndvi_clean` | NDVI only where reliability is good or marginal. **Use this one.** |

#### `modis_et.csv` — MODIS MOD16A2GF, 8-day, 500 m (optional, ORNL)

`et_mm_8day` is actual ET summed over the composite and `pet_mm_8day` is potential ET. `et_ratio = ET/PET`: below ~0.5 the crop is water-limited, near 1 it is unstressed. This product **does** see irrigation, unlike POWER.

#### `modis_lst.csv` — MODIS MOD11A2, 8-day, 1 km (optional, ORNL)

`lst_day_c` is daytime land-surface temperature, the leaf and soil "skin" heat. It runs hotter than air temperature. This is the demo's "heat stress" source.

#### `landcover_annual.csv` — MODIS MCD12Q1, yearly (optional, ORNL)

`igbp_class`: 10 = grassland, 12 = cropland, 13 = urban, 14 = cropland/natural mosaic.

#### `soil_profile.csv` — SoilGrids, long format

One row per property × depth (11 × 6 = 66 rows), with `mean`, `q05` and `q95` (90% prediction interval). Properties: `bdod` bulk density, `cec`, `cfvo` coarse fragments, `clay`, `sand`, `silt`, `nitrogen`, `phh2o` pH, `soc` organic carbon, `wv0033` field capacity, `wv1500` wilting point.

#### `soil_summary.json`

Depth-weighted 0–30 cm topsoil values, USDA texture class, organic matter %, and available water capacity (mm in the top metre). It also includes `app_labels` in the exact vocabulary of the app's `SoilProfile` type.

#### `terrain.json` ✅

`elevation_m`, `slope_pct_field` (100 m scale), `slope_pct_landscape_mean` (~1.5 km), `relief_m_within_1500m` and `erosion_risk_from_slope`.

#### Built by `05_build_summaries.mjs` ✅

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `monthly_series.csv` | year × month (288 rows): temperature, rain, ET0, water balance, soil wetness, heat/heavy-rain days, GDD, plus NDVI, MODIS ET and LST once fetched |
| `monthly_climatology.csv` | the 12-month typical year. Adds `ndvi_p10`/`ndvi_p90` and `typically_bare` (mean NDVI < 0.3). |
| `annual.csv` | 24 rows: `heat_days`, `precip_mm`, `heavy_rain_days`, `max_dry_spell_days`, `precip_p95_day_mm`, `gdd`, `et0_mm`, `water_balance_mm`, `soil_wet_top_mean`, NDVI/ET/LST summaries, … |
| `weather_daily.csv` | the **merged** daily series everything else is computed from: rain (CHIRPS → POWER → ERA5), temperature (POWER, or ERA5 where POWER is broken), recomputed crop water demand, water balance, degree days, heat and downpour flags |
| `trends.json` | `trends`: for every annual metric, OLS slope per decade, first-10-year vs last-10-year means, % change, Mann-Kendall z and p. `quality`: `weather` (`ok`/`suspect`, judged on the merged series), `rain_source`, `temp_source`, `ndvi_source`, `power_check` (POWER's own 2008 step test) and `final_check`. **Check `quality.weather` before using any weather number.** |

✅ = fetched for all regions. ⏳ = not fetched for the new regions yet (MODIS is slow: `02_fetch_modis.mjs --only=ndvi`).

### Crop scores from measured data — `processed/scored/` (built by `05b_score_measured.py`)

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `sowcast_region_crop_fit.csv` | Same columns as the draft file, but scored by the team's own rules (`make_data.py`) using measured rain, temperature, dry months, topsoil and slope. `trend` is filled: each crop is re-scored year by year (2001–2024) and a Mann-Kendall test (p < 0.10 and at least a 2-point change) gives `increasing`, `declining` or `flat`. |
| `sowcast_rotations.csv` | Rotation table recomputed from those scores |
| `region_inputs_used.csv` | For every region and input: `draft`, `measured`, which was `used`, and the `source`. Dry months = months where rain is below half the crop water demand. For literature-sourced regions (Dry Zone, Isan) the draft topsoil is kept, and SoilGrids is shown for comparison. |

## 3. App files — `public/data/` (generated by `06_build_app_data.mjs`; do not edit)

Shapes are defined in `src/types/index.ts`.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `crops.json` | the 21 crops from `sowcast_crops.csv` |
| `regions/index.json` | 31 region summaries for the map menu: name, reference town, country, lat/lon, tagline (common crops + rain), confidence |
| `regions/<id>.json` | everything one region's pages need (below) |

| Field in `regions/<id>.json` | Comes from | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `monthly`, `annual`, `trends` | processed POWER tables | trend stats are emptied when `data_quality.weather = suspect` |
| `data_quality` | `trends.json → quality` | `weather` (hides weather cards and charts when `suspect`) and `sources` (which rain, temperature and greenness source the region uses) |
| `scoring_inputs` | `scored/region_inputs_used.csv` | shown on the Brief as "Team estimate vs measured" |
| `soil` | `soil_summary.json` + `terrain.json` | `sampled_km_from_town` = distance from the town point where soil data was found |
| `priors` | `sowcast_regions.csv` | shown as "Team draft values, not yet measured" |
| `insights` | rules in `06_build_app_data.mjs` | up to 3: significant POWER trends (p < 0.15), water budget, best-matched crops, draft salinity/flood risks |
| `rotations` | `sowcast_rotations.csv` + crop traits | 1–5 scores derived from crop traits; `cover_by_month` is `null` until crop calendars exist |
| `crop_fit` | `scored/sowcast_region_crop_fit.csv` (falls back to the draft file) | `trend` from the year-by-year re-scoring; `unknown` where weather isn't reliable |
