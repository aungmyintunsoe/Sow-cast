# Context — the data behind Sowcast

## Scope decisions

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|
| 2026-11-14 | **Regions:** the team's 23 regions in Myanmar (6), Thailand (6), Vietnam (7) and Cambodia (4), from `inputs/sowcast_regions.csv`. The original four demo regions (Iowa, Punjab, Nakuru, Darling Downs), their crops and the fake-data generator were deleted. |
| 2026-11-14 | **Open data only:** no NASA Earthdata-login sources (SMAP, GPM IMERG, ECOSTRESS, GRACE-FO, HLS). So there is no groundwater data; the app shows a water-budget card instead. |
| 2026-11-14 | **Map:** plant greenness only (NASA GIBS MODIS Terra NDVI, newest 8-day image via the `default` date, so it never goes stale). Regions are picked from a country accordion or by tapping the map. True-colour imagery and label layers were removed. |
| 2026-11-14 | **Design:** the original Field colours with a thin dark card outline. |

## What is draft and what is measured

The app shows both and labels which is which.

| | Draft (team, `inputs/`) | Measured (open data, `processed/`) |
|---|---|---|
| Rain, temperature | `rain_mm_approx`, `mean_temp_c_approx` | NASA POWER 2001–2024 (good for 15 regions; broken for 8) |
| Soil, slope | `soil_texture`, `drainage`, `slope_pct_approx` | SoilGrids, SRTM |
| Flood, salinity, irrigation | 0–3 scores | not yet (NEXT_STEPS.md B6–B8) |
| Crop fit (0–95) | rule-based score in `make_data.py` | will use measured climate after step A2 |
| Rotations | 69 options, 6 literature-backed | scores derived from crop traits by `06_build_app_data.mjs` |

## Key findings so far

1. **POWER weather has a 2008 break in 8 regions** (all of Myanmar, Vietnam's Central Highlands, Cambodia's plateau): rain ×2–10 and temperature −0.4 to −1.9 °C at the same moment. It is caught automatically, and the app hides weather numbers there.
2. **Where POWER is good, the team's draft rain is close** (within 15% for 11 of 15 regions). The exception is Ninh Thuan (South Central Coast), where the draft 700 mm is probably right and POWER's 1,478 mm is a coarse-grid artefact.
3. **Reference points are town centres.** Fine for ~50 km weather, wrong for soil and greenness. Soil falls back to points 3–6 km away; moving points onto cropland is planned (B4).
4. **Wetter almost everywhere the weather data is usable.** Of the 15 regions that passed the check, 9 show a clear (Mann-Kendall p < 0.05) rise in yearly rain or downpour days (e.g. Mekong Delta 1,612 → 1,992 mm; Red River Delta 1,484 → 1,925 mm). Tonle Sap shows more very hot days. Five regions show no clear change: North and Isan in Thailand, Vietnam's Southeast, and Cambodia's Mekong Plain and Coastal. A region-wide wetting signal from a single reanalysis can be drift, so confirm it with CHIRPS (NEXT_STEPS.md B1) before calling it a finding.

## Design choices in the pipeline

- **Node with no dependencies for fetching**; Python (`data/.venv`) for analysis and `make_data.py`.
- **The raw API cache is git-ignored; processed tables and inputs are committed.**
- **Every sentence the app shows is rule-based and names its source and confidence** (`06_build_app_data.mjs`). Nothing is generated by an AI model at runtime.
