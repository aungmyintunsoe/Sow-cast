# Context — the data stage of Sowcast

## Where we are

The prototype app (`src/`) runs on **fake data** generated by `scripts/make_fake_data.ts` and stored in `public/data/regions/*.json`. This `data/` folder is the stage that replaces it: a reproducible pipeline that pulls **real NASA and partner data** for the four demo regions and shapes it for analysis and, later, for the app.

| Region | App pin | Sampled field point | Why moved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story County, Iowa | 42.03, −93.62 | 42.10, −93.55 | Pin is on Ames (urban) |
| Ludhiana, Punjab | 30.90, 75.85 | 30.80, 75.70 | Pin is on Ludhiana city |
| Nakuru, Kenya | −0.30, 36.07 | −0.27, 35.80 | Pin is on Nakuru town |
| Darling Downs, Qld | −27.56, 151.95 | −27.5854, 151.5134 | Pin is on Toowoomba; auto-moved to the nearest MODIS cropland pixel |

> **Finding #1:** all four app pins sit on towns (MODIS land cover class 13 = urban). Satellite crop data sampled at the pins would describe rooftops. The app's map pins can stay where they are for display, but **all data is sampled at the `field` points** in `config/sites.json`.

## What the data already says (preliminary — POWER weather only, 2001–2024)

| Site | Typical year | Strongest change (Mann-Kendall) |
|---|---|---|
| **Iowa** | 840 mm rain, 144 frost days, 6 heat days | Longest dry spell 20 → 26 days (p = 0.02). Frost-free season +7 days/decade (p = 0.03). |
| **Punjab** | 715 mm rain, 151 days ≥ 35 °C, ~1,040 mm/yr deficit | Rain 564 → 894 mm and cooler (p < 0.001). ⚠ Treat as a hypothesis: it may reflect irrigation cooling, and it needs IMERG/IMD confirmation. |
| **Nakuru** | 1,390 mm rain (likely high), no heat or frost days, 15% slope | Heavy-rain days 6 → 16 per year (p = 0.01). Wettest days ~50% heavier (p = 0.003). |
| **Darling Downs** | 580 mm rain, 40 heat days, ~1,080 mm/yr deficit | Frost days falling (p = 0.003), but the counts are tiny and likely under-counted. |

Not yet known: groundwater (needs GRACE-FO), measured soil moisture (SMAP), field-scale heat (ECOSTRESS), and NDVI/ET until the MODIS download completes.

## Design decisions (and why)

- **Node, zero dependencies.** It runs on the Node already used for the app. Python wasn't installed, and no `npm install` is needed.
- **Keyless first.** Everything that runs now needs no account. Login-only NASA sources are inventoried (`data_availability.csv`) and have a ready script.
- **SoilGrids instead of SSURGO.** One global source treats all four countries equally.
- **Cache everything raw, commit only processed.** Re-runs are free, and the team analyses identical numbers.
- **Output in the app's own shape** (`region_climate.json` ≈ `RegionDetail`), so integration is a merge, not a rewrite.
- **Honesty flags built in:** POWER model ET renamed `et_model_mm`, SRTM slope computed at 100 m (30 m was noise), and soil caveats stored inside the JSON.
